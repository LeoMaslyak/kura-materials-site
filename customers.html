What You Buy, How It's Made

The simple answer to the most-asked question: we replace aggregates (not cement) with processed mining tailings — verified, safe, and structurally sound.

📋 The Material Question — Answered

What's in the concrete?

Our Eco-Concrete replaces up to 70% of the fine and coarse aggregates (sand, gravel) with processed mining tailings. The cement binder remains standard OPC or blended cement.

Tailings are the finely ground rock left after ore extraction. After processing (removing heavy metals via bioleaching), they behave like high-quality silica-rich aggregates.

Do we replace cement?

In our standard Eco-Concrete: No — cement stays standard. The savings come from replacing virgin aggregate.

In our Geopolymer Binder products: Yes — we also replace Portland cement with an alkali-activated slag binder, cutting cement use by 50–80% and CO₂ by up to 80%.

Aggregate replacement → 60% CO₂ reduction | Cement replacement → up to 80% CO₂ reduction

Technical FAQ

Real answers to the questions architects, engineers and procurement teams ask.

It depends on the product line. In our Eco-Concrete and Recycled Aggregate products, we replace the aggregate (sand and gravel) — not the cement. Up to 70% of the aggregate fraction is substituted with processed mining tailings. Your cement type and dosage stays the same.

In our Geopolymer Binder product, we go further and also replace 50–80% of the Portland cement with an alkali-activated slag binder. This is the highest-carbon-reduction option, cutting embodied CO₂ by up to 80%.

Mining tailings are the finely ground rock remaining after ore extraction — typically 60–80% silica, alumina, and iron oxide. After extraction of residual metals (via our bioleaching process), the silica-rich fraction is the main component we use.

The silica (SiO₂) and alumina (Al₂O₃) fractions are excellent natural pozzolans and aggregates. They are chemically inert once processed, well-graded, and comply with EN 12620 particle size standards for concrete aggregate.

Yes, raw tailings contain trace heavy metals (copper, zinc, lead, arsenic depending on the ore type). Our processing step — bioleaching using naturally occurring microorganisms — selectively extracts these metals before the material is used in construction.

All finished materials are tested by independent labs and certified to comply with EU heavy metal limit values for construction materials. Test certificates are provided with every delivery.

We provide Environmental Product Declarations (EPD) for Eco-Concrete and Paving Block products. EPDs quantify embodied CO₂ per m³ / per tonne and are required by most LEED / BREEAM projects.
