The global concrete market exceeds $400 billion annually, while mining operations manage billions of tons of tailings worldwide. By transforming tailings into construction materials, we unlock a scalable opportunity at the intersection of mining waste management and sustainable infrastructure.

The Problem

⛏️

Mining's Hidden Cost

Mining operations generate 8 billion+ tons of tailings every year — creating environmental risks, land use challenges, and long-term storage costs that run into billions of dollars.

  • ✓ Contamination of soil and groundwater
  • ✓ Dam failure risks (e.g. Brumadinho disaster)
  • ✓ Billions in annual remediation costs
🏭

Concrete's Carbon Crisis

Concrete is the most widely used building material, yet its production generates nearly 8% of global CO₂ emissions. Regulatory pressure and ESG mandates are forcing the industry to change.

  • ✓ EU Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism
  • ✓ Green building mandates accelerating
  • ✓ ESG reporting now required for public companies
