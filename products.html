How Tailings Become Products

Our proprietary process transforms environmental liabilities into high-performance construction materials

1

Source & Collect

Mining tailings — the byproduct of mineral extraction — are sourced from copper and iron operations. 200 tons of waste per 1 ton of ore extracted.

2

Alkali Activation

Proprietary alkali-activation chemistry converts inert waste into engineered aggregates and binding agents. Up to 70% cement replacement achieved.

3

Manufacture & Deliver

Precision molds and industrial curing produce 7 categories of construction-grade materials — concrete, aggregates, geopolymers, furniture, and more.
